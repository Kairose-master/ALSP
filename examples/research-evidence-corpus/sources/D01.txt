The fictional Lantern archive pilot indexes scanned theatre programmes. This configuration applies only to pilot lane L; the production archive is outside its scope.
For pilot lane L, the approved shard limit is 64 MiB as of 2026-04-01. MiB means 1,048,576 bytes; it does not mean decimal MB.
Dictionary compression is not enabled for pilot lane L. A smaller shard limit alone does not enable it.
The dashboard uses violet for queued work, amber for work in progress, and slate for completed work. These colours describe interface state only and are not QA approval indicators.
The meeting room display rotates through three sample programme covers. Their decorative sequence is alphabetic by show title and has no relationship to the order used by the indexing jobs.
